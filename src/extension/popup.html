Fobles

  1. Set Site access in the browser's extension controls.
  2. Open a page on an approved Sitecore domain.
  3. In Chrome Extensions Shortcuts, verify or assign Ctrl+Shift+E for Toggle Fobles.

Open chrome://extensions/shortcuts to manage shortcuts. On macOS, use Command+Shift+E.
